How It Works

01

Scan & fingerprint

App reads SSID, MAC address, signal strength, and security type for every nearby network.

02

Score with ML

A model trained on labeled Wi-Fi attack data (AWID/AWID3) flags duplicate names, weak encryption, and odd signal behavior.

03

Guide & protect

You get a plain-language reason for the score, a safer alternative nearby, and a prompt to enable a VPN if needed.
