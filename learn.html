Quick Glossary
Evil TwinA fake Wi-Fi hotspot made to look exactly like a real one, set up to trick your phone into connecting.
Man-in-the-MiddleWhen someone secretly sits between you and the internet, able to see what passes through.
Open NetworkWi-Fi with no password — anyone nearby can potentially read what's sent over it.
VPNA tool that scrambles your data into a private tunnel, so even a risky network can't read it.
