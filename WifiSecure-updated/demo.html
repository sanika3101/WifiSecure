What can this network see?

Without HTTPS: some web activity and data may be exposed depending on the application.

With HTTPS: website content and credentials are encrypted in transit. HTTPS protects the application connection; it does not prove that the Wi-Fi network itself is trustworthy.

VPN: a trusted VPN can encrypt traffic between your device and the VPN server, but it does not prove the Wi-Fi is legitimate.

WifiSecure provides a risk assessment based on available indicators. It cannot guarantee that a network is completely safe. Always verify trusted networks independently and use HTTPS and other security best practices.
